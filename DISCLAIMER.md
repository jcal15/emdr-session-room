## Health and safety risks

- Bilateral stimulation and trauma processing can bring up distressing memories, strong emotions, panic, dissociation or other reactions, during or after use.
- The moving and flashing light may trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy and may cause dizziness, eye strain, headaches or nausea. Do not use it if you have a seizure disorder or are sensitive to flashing or moving light, unless your doctor says it is safe.
- Keep headphone volume at a safe, comfortable level.
- Never use it while driving, operating machinery, or in any situation where reduced awareness could be dangerous.
- The client screen intentionally has no stop button; sets are started and stopped by the person using the therapist controls. A client can end a session at any moment by closing the page, locking the device or removing headphones.
